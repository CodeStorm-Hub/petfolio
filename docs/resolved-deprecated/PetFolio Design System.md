# PetFolio — Design System Specification

**Version:** 1.0 · **Platform:** Flutter (iOS / Android / Web)
**Author:** Design Systems · **Status:** Draft for review

> PetFolio is a pet super‑app spanning social feed, pet matching, health & care tracking, and marketplace. The design system must hold up across all four pillars — feeling expressive and warm in social/discovery contexts, calm and trustworthy in health contexts, and crisp and commercial in marketplace contexts.

---

## 1. Design Principles

1. **Warm, not saccharine.** Pets are emotional — colors and copy should feel affectionate, but the system stays grown‑up. No cartoon mascots in the chrome.
2. **Outdoor‑first contrast.** Most owners use the app outside, often in direct sun, with one hand, while a leash is tugging. Every contrast and touch target is sized for that worst case.
3. **Calm in health, confident in commerce, playful in social.** One palette, three "rooms." Tonal shifts (not new color families) communicate which surface the user is on.
4. **Adaptive density.** The same layouts must work for a 4.7" phone in glare and a 12.9" tablet on the couch. Type and spacing scale together, never independently.
5. **Glass is a moment, not a uniform.** Glassmorphism is reserved for hero/floating contexts (the live walk widget, the active match card, the cart drawer). Body surfaces are solid for legibility.

### 2.5 Outdoor Contrast Rules

Walking‑a‑dog conditions: bright ambient light, screen brightness clipped, polarized sunglasses, motion. We exceed WCAG and target **APCA Lc** instead:

| Context                              | Min APCA Lc | WCAG fallback |
| ------------------------------------ | ----------- | ------------- |
| Body text on surface                 | **Lc 75**   | 7.0 : 1       |
| Primary button label on `blue/500`   | **Lc 75**   | 7.0 : 1       |
| Secondary text / captions            | **Lc 60**   | 4.5 : 1       |
| Icons used as the sole affordance    | **Lc 60**   | 4.5 : 1       |
| Disabled / decorative                | Lc 45       | 3.0 : 1       |

**Outdoor Mode** (auto‑triggered when ambient light sensor > 10 000 lux, or user‑toggled from the walk widget) applies these adjustments globally:

- Surface drops to `#FFFFFF` (light) or rises to `#0A0F1C` (dark) — pure ends of the ramp, no tint.
- Body text snaps to `ink/950`.
- Primary button gains a 1.5 px `ink/950` outer ring at 35 % opacity for edge definition against bright concrete/grass.
- Glassmorphism is **disabled**; all `glass.*` surfaces fall back to their `solid.*` equivalents (see §4.1).
- Icon stroke width increases from 1.75 px to 2.25 px.
- All shadows drop to 50 % opacity (sun washes them out anyway; less GPU).

## 3. Typography

### 3.1 Type Pairing

- **Display & UI:** *Sora* — geometric, friendly, slightly rounded terminals. Wide x‑height for outdoor legibility.
- **Body & Numerics:** *Inter* (with `cv11`, `tnum`, `ss01` features enabled) — proven UI workhorse, tabular figures for health charts and prices.
- **Monospace (data only):** *JetBrains Mono* — vet record IDs, order numbers.

If a script lacks Sora coverage (e.g. CJK, Arabic), fall back to Noto Sans of the matching script at the same weight; *never* substitute Inter for headlines — the visual rhythm depends on the Sora/Inter contrast.

### 3.2 Adaptive Type Scale

Type scales with **viewport width AND user accessibility size**. Each role declares a `min → preferred → max` range. The preferred value is a fluid `clamp()` between two breakpoints (360 px and 1280 px).

| Role          | Weight | Mobile (min) | Mobile (default) | Tablet      | Line height | Tracking |
| ------------- | ------ | ------------ | ---------------- | ----------- | ----------- | -------- |
| Display XL    | 700    | 32 / 38      | 36 / 42          | 56 / 62     | 1.05        | −1.5 %   |
| Display       | 700    | 26 / 32      | 30 / 36          | 44 / 50     | 1.08        | −1 %     |
| Headline      | 600    | 22 / 28      | 24 / 30          | 32 / 38     | 1.15        | −0.5 %   |
| Title         | 600    | 18 / 24      | 20 / 26          | 24 / 30     | 1.2         | 0        |
| Body L        | 400    | 16 / 22      | 17 / 24          | 18 / 26     | 1.45        | 0        |
| **Body** ★    | 400    | 15 / 22      | 16 / 24          | 16 / 24     | 1.5         | 0        |
| Body S        | 400    | 13 / 18      | 14 / 20          | 14 / 20     | 1.45        | +0.5 %   |
| Caption       | 500    | 12 / 16      | 12 / 16          | 12 / 16     | 1.35        | +1.5 %   |
| Overline      | 600    | 11 / 14      | 11 / 14          | 11 / 14     | 1.2         | +8 %     |
| Numeric (tab) | 500    | 16 / 22      | 17 / 24          | 18 / 26     | 1.2         | 0        |

★ **Floor rule:** Body never renders below 15 px, even when the system text‑size slider is set to its smallest. Outdoor Mode raises the floor to 16 px.

### 3.3 Dynamic Type Behavior

- Honors **iOS Dynamic Type** and **Android font scale** up to 200 %.
- Above 130 % scale, layouts re‑flow: side‑by‑side button rows stack vertically; bottom‑sheet `Body L` truncation is removed and the sheet grows.
- Display roles cap at 150 % scale to prevent overflow on hero cards; everything else scales linearly.
- Numeric token is **never** scaled by the slider for charts and prices — it scales with the chart's own zoom instead, so health graphs stay aligned.

### 3.4 Type Color Rules

- Headlines: `ink/950`.
- Body: `ink/700` for prose, `ink/950` for actionable copy (form labels, button-adjacent text).
- On `blue/500` and saturated secondaries: pure white `#FFFFFF`, never `surface/0`.
- On glass surfaces: always `ink/950` (light) / `surface/0` (dark) — the tints below glass will pull medium‑contrast text below APCA Lc 75.

## 4. Surfaces

### 4.1 Glassmorphism Cards

Glass is used for **floating, transient, or hero** surfaces only:

- The live walk widget (overlays the map).
- The active match card (overlays the photo).
- The cart preview drawer.
- Toasts and the now‑playing audio note bar.

It is **never** used for:

- Health record cards (legibility & trust).
- Marketplace product cards (commerce expects opacity).
- Form fields or inputs.
- Anything that may appear over a busy/light background without a fallback.

#### Glass token

| Property                    | Light                                              | Dark                                                |
| --------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------- |
| Fill                        | `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.62)`                        | `rgba(20, 27, 45, 0.55)`                            |
| Backdrop blur               | 24 px Gaussian, 1.4× saturation                    | 28 px Gaussian, 1.2× saturation                     |
| Inner border (top‑inset)    | 1 px `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.55)` (highlight)       | 1 px `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.10)`                    |
| Outer border                | 1 px `rgba(11, 18, 32, 0.06)`                      | 1 px `rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.40)`                          |
| Drop shadow                 | `0 16 40 −8 rgba(11,18,32,0.18)`                   | `0 18 48 −10 rgba(0,0,0,0.55)`                      |
| Corner radius               | `xl` = 20 px (small glass) / `2xl` = 28 px (sheet) | same                                                |
| Tint layer (optional)       | `blue/50` at 20 % over fill                        | `blue/900` at 18 % over fill                        |
| Specular highlight          | 8 % white linear‑gradient, top 30 % only           | 4 % white linear‑gradient, top 30 % only            |

#### Required behind‑guarantees

Glass is allowed only when the layer behind it satisfies **one** of:

1. A photographic image (live preview, pet photo, map).
2. A solid block of a **neutral or blue** token.
3. A bottom‑sheet scrim of `rgba(11, 18, 32, 0.35)` or stronger.

If none are guaranteed (e.g. a glass widget pinned on the social feed scroll), the component **automatically swaps to its solid fallback**: `surface/0` fill, 1 px `line/200` border, same shadow halved. The same swap fires in **Outdoor Mode** unconditionally.

#### Accessibility

- Reduce Transparency (iOS) / Remove Animations (Android): glass swaps to solid fallback.
- Body text on glass must use the un‑tinted ink token; do not place secondary text on glass below 14 px.
- Never stack two glass surfaces; the second swaps to solid.

### 4.2 Solid Card

The workhorse. Used for feed posts, product tiles, vaccination records, etc.

- Fill: `surface/0`.
- Border: 1 px `line/200` (light) / `line/200` dark token.
- Radius: `lg` = 16 px default, `md` = 12 px for compact list items.
- Shadow: `0 1 2 0 rgba(11,18,32,0.04)`, plus `0 8 24 −8 rgba(11,18,32,0.08)` on lift/hover.
- Inset padding: 16 px on phones, 20 px on tablets.

### 4.3 Elevation Scale

| Level | Use                                  | Light shadow                                                                   | Dark shadow                                |
| ----- | ------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------ |
| `e0`  | App background                       | none                                                                           | none                                       |
| `e1`  | Card                                 | `0 1 2 0 rgba(11,18,32,0.04)`                                                  | `0 1 2 0 rgba(0,0,0,0.35)`                 |
| `e2`  | Card hover / sticky header           | `0 4 12 −2 rgba(11,18,32,0.10)`                                                | `0 6 14 −2 rgba(0,0,0,0.45)`               |
| `e3`  | Bottom sheet, popover                | `0 12 28 −6 rgba(11,18,32,0.14)`                                               | `0 14 32 −6 rgba(0,0,0,0.55)`              |
| `e4`  | Modal, FAB                           | `0 20 40 −8 rgba(11,18,32,0.20)`                                               | `0 24 48 −10 rgba(0,0,0,0.65)`             |

### 4.4 Radius Scale

`xs` 6 · `sm` 8 · `md` 12 · `lg` 16 · `xl` 20 · `2xl` 28 · `pill` 999.

Pet photos always use `xl`; product photos always use `lg`; avatars are `pill`. This is intentional — circular product shots read as "social," not "shoppable."

## 5. Buttons & Touch Targets

### 5.1 Sizing Philosophy

Outdoor, gloved, leash‑in‑hand use means **44 × 44 dp is the floor, never the target**. We design to **52 dp minimum** for any primary affordance and **64 dp** for the walk‑mode primary.

| Size token         | Height | Min width | Horizontal pad | Label role | Use                                                  |
| ------------------ | ------ | --------- | -------------- | ---------- | ---------------------------------------------------- |
| `btn/sm`           | 36 dp  | 72 dp     | 14 dp          | Body S 600 | Inline secondary in dense lists                      |
| `btn/md`           | 44 dp  | 96 dp     | 16 dp          | Body 600   | Default secondary                                    |
| **`btn/lg`** ★     | 52 dp  | 120 dp    | 20 dp          | Body L 600 | **Default primary** — buy, save, schedule, match     |
| `btn/xl`           | 60 dp  | 160 dp    | 24 dp          | Title 600  | Sheet primary, onboarding CTA                        |
| `btn/walk`         | 64 dp  | full‑width or 64 sq | 24 dp | Title 600 | Outdoor & walk‑mode actions, "End walk", SOS         |

★ Hit area is always **expanded to 48 dp minimum** via invisible padding, even when the visual is smaller (e.g. a `btn/sm` chip).

### 5.2 Variants

Each variant has resting / hover / pressed / focus / disabled / loading states.

- **Primary (filled)** — `blue/500` fill, white label, `e1` shadow → `e2` on press. Pressed scales to 0.97 with 80 ms ease‑out spring; this is the *only* button that scales (haptic anchor).
- **Secondary (tonal)** — `blue/100` fill (light) / `blue/100` dark token, `blue/700` label. No shadow.
- **Ghost (text)** — transparent, `blue/600` label, 8 % `blue/500` hover tint.
- **Destructive** — `danger` fill, white label. Reserved for irreversible actions (delete pet profile, cancel order).
- **Glass primary** — for floating overlays (walk widget): glass token + 1 px `rgba(255,255,255,0.4)` inner border + white label with 0 1 0 `rgba(0,0,0,0.3)` text shadow for outdoor legibility.
- **Icon‑only** — must always carry an `aria‑label` (or Flutter `Semantics.label`); minimum 44 dp tap target with a 12 dp icon centered.

### 5.3 Focus & Feedback

- Focus ring: 2 px `blue/400` offset 2 px outside the button border. Always visible to keyboard / switch users; suppressed for touch via `:focus-visible` equivalent.
- Press feedback: filled buttons get a haptic *light impact*; destructive gets *medium*; `btn/walk` gets *medium* + 120 ms ring pulse to confirm in noisy environments.
- Loading: label is replaced by a 20 dp spinner; button width is locked to prevent layout shift. Disabled inputs blocked.

### 5.4 Spacing in Button Groups

- Adjacent buttons: 12 dp gap (mobile), 16 dp gap (tablet).
- Primary always sits on the right in left‑to‑right locales; below the secondary in stacked layout.
- When more than two buttons stack, demote all but the primary to ghost.

## 6. Spacing & Layout

### 6.1 Spacing Scale (4 dp base)

`0 · 2 · 4 · 6 · 8 · 12 · 16 · 20 · 24 · 32 · 40 · 56 · 80 · 120`

Tokens: `space/0` through `space/13`. Components reference tokens, never raw dp.

### 6.2 Grid

- **Mobile (≤599 dp):** 4‑column, 16 dp gutters, 16 dp margins.
- **Tablet portrait (600–899 dp):** 8‑column, 20 dp gutters, 24 dp margins.
- **Tablet landscape / desktop web (≥900 dp):** 12‑column, 24 dp gutters, 32 dp margins. Content max‑width 1200 dp.

### 6.3 Safe Areas

- Top: status bar + 12 dp.
- Bottom: home indicator + the tab bar (when present) + 12 dp.
- Walk mode reserves an additional 96 dp bottom inset for the persistent "End walk" `btn/walk`; this offset is broadcast to all child scrolls.

## 7. Iconography

- **Stroke** style, 1.75 px stroke at 24 dp, round caps, round joins. Outdoor Mode → 2.25 px stroke.
- Two weights: **regular** (UI) and **filled** (selected tab, liked, owned).
- Pet‑class icons (dog, cat, bird, reptile, fish, small mammal) are bespoke and ship in both stroke and filled variants. Everything else comes from a single consistent library — never mix sets.
- Icon color follows text color of the row by default; only category icons carry their own warm secondary color.

## 8. Motion

- **Durations:** `xs` 80 ms · `sm` 140 ms · `md` 220 ms · `lg` 320 ms · `xl` 500 ms.
- **Easings:** `standard` cubic‑bezier(0.2, 0, 0, 1) · `enter` (0, 0, 0, 1) · `exit` (0.4, 0, 1, 1) · `spring/soft` 0.6 damping / 18 stiffness.
- Page transitions: shared‑element for pet → pet profile, product → product details; otherwise a 220 ms slide+fade.
- Like, match, and milestone confirmations use a single, restrained 320 ms spring scale — no confetti, no looping shimmers.
- Honors **Reduce Motion**: all non‑essential motion becomes 80 ms opacity fades; shared‑element transitions become cross‑fades.

## 9. Pillar Tonality

The same components shift accent and elevation slightly depending on the active pillar. This is the *only* place the system theming varies.

| Pillar       | Accent (secondary) | Surface bias        | Default elevation | Tone                  |
| ------------ | ------------------ | ------------------- | ----------------- | --------------------- |
| Social       | `sunset/500`       | `surface/1`         | `e1`              | Lively, photographic  |
| Match        | `coral/500`        | photo full‑bleed    | `e3` on cards     | Warm, immersive       |
| Health       | `meadow/500`       | `surface/0`         | `e1`, lots of whitespace | Calm, clinical |
| Marketplace  | `apricot/500`      | `surface/0`         | `e2` on tiles     | Crisp, commercial     |

Primary `blue/500` is the **only** color shared across all pillars — it is what makes the app feel like one product.

## 10. Accessibility Checklist (per release)

- All interactive elements ≥ 48 dp hit target.
- APCA Lc thresholds met for every text/background pairing in §2.5.
- VoiceOver / TalkBack labels present on every icon‑only button and every photo (pet name + alt text).
- Reduce Motion + Reduce Transparency fallbacks render correctly across every pillar.
- Outdoor Mode visually verified in 10 000 lux test rig on the two reference devices (iPhone 14, Pixel 8).
- Dynamic Type at 200 % does not clip or overlap any primary flow.
- Color is never the sole carrier of meaning (status pills always pair with an icon or label).

## 11. Tokens (delivery format)

Tokens are authored in a single `tokens.json` (W3C Design Tokens draft format) and transformed into:

- Flutter `ThemeData` + `ColorScheme` + custom extensions for glass, walk, pillar.
- Figma variables (Light / Dark / Outdoor modes).
- A static reference site for QA.

Naming convention: `category/role/state` — e.g. `color.button.primary.pressed`, `space/4`, `radius.xl`, `motion.duration.md`. Components consume tokens; raw values are never written in product code.
